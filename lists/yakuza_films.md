# Japanese Yakuza Films

Complete list of 99 Japanese yakuza films with verified IMDb IDs, spanning 1948–2021.
Covers classic Toei ninkyo eiga, 1970s jitsuroku, Seijun Suzuki's Nikkatsu output,
Takeshi Kitano, Takashi Miike, the full Battles Without Honor and Humanity saga,
Tai Kato's crime films, Sadao Nakajima's Japanese Godfather trilogy, and modern entries.

Duplicates between sections have been collapsed. Original Japanese titles are in Notes
as the most reliable disambiguator for the many same-title series entries.

## Notes

**Excluded deliberately:**
- Tokyo Godfathers (2003) — Satoshi Kon anime, not a yakuza film
- Kikujiro (1999) — Kitano plays an ex-yakuza but the film isn't yakuza-centered
- Radiance's The Betrayal (tt0123028) — Daiei samurai film, not yakuza

**Not Radiance:** Outlaw: Gangster VIP, Retaliation, Bloody Territories and the
Stray Cat Rock films are Arrow Video releases.

**Same-title traps handled:** Graveyard of Honor appears twice by design (1975
Fukasaku tt0073207 vs 2002 Miike tt0316599). The Battles saga is split across the
original pentalogy, the New Battles trilogy, and three later-director entries.

**Spot-check after import:** #45 Police Tactics, #89 Gonin, #95 Last of the Wolves —
all verified but their IMDb pages use variant English titles.

**Total entries: 95.**
