## Notes on specific entries

**Dual entries (movie #108):** The Pokémon Black/White twin films each have their own IMDb page. **Victini and Reshiram** is tt1961324; **Victini and Zekrom** is tt1739212.

**Shared entry (movie #94):** Final Fantasy VII: Advent Children Complete (2009) does not have a separate IMDb page. It shares **tt0385700** with the original 2005 release.

**Minor year discrepancies:** A handful of films show slightly different years on IMDb versus the list provided, typically due to differences between festival premieres, Japanese theatrical release, and international release dates. These include Ninja Gaiden OVA (IMDb: 1991), Dragon Quest: Dai no Daibōken – Tachiagare!! Aban no Shito (IMDb: 1992), Tekken live action (IMDb: 2010), Dead Trigger (IMDb: 2017), and Monster Strike: Sora no Kanata (IMDb: 2018). The tt IDs provided are confirmed correct for the intended films.

**Series/mini-series classifications:** Several titles—including Halo 4: Forward Unto Dawn (tt2262308), Halo: Nightfall (tt3804114), and Street Fighter: Assassin's Fist (tt3069212)—are classified on IMDb as TV Mini Series rather than standalone films, as they originated as episodic digital content later compiled into feature-length releases. The provided IDs are their primary IMDb entries.
